01 The workflow problem

Why are we still redrawing the exact same garments every season?

The industry reality

Most collections are built on the exact same garment foundations — t-shirts, hoodies, pants, and standard silhouettes.

Yet development teams still spend hours rebuilding technical flats manually, anchor point by anchor point, version after version.

The template pain point

Let's be honest: most free fashion flat templates are completely unreliable. Bad proportions, broken vector groups, and random construction.

They are not built for professional manufacturing workflows — they are built to be downloaded and abandoned.

The software gap

The alternative isn't much better. Professional fashion CAD tools are overly complex, wildly expensive, and require bloated enterprise suites.

Free templates solve speed, but ruin consistency.

The missing middle layer

FlatLabs delivers technically correct fashion flats built for everyday garment production. You get reusable base constructions, editable SVG vectors, and clean specs designed to move faster from concept to clothing factory.

Patternmaker positioning

To be clear: custom pattern engineering, specialized draping, and advanced silhouette development still belong with master patternmakers.

FlatLabs handles the standardized layer underneath — the technical foundations most collections already rely on — so development starts further downstream, not from zero.

Professional fashion CAD tools are often complex, expensive, or oversized for everyday flat work. Free templates solve speed, but not consistency.

FlatLabs was built right between those two extremes.
